=>Fluent Strings

Chaining of methods in string.

$info = "hi, how are you";

Normal -------

$info = Str::ucfirst(($info));
$info = Str::replaceFirst('Hi', 'hello', $info);
$info = Str::camel(($info));

Fluent -------

$info = Str::of($info)
    ->ucfirst($info)
    ->replaceFirst('Hi', 'hello', $info)
    ->camel($info);

echo $info;
